# CAFÉ — Related Work & Reviewer-Critique Research

_Deep-research sweep: **70 unique adjacent papers** (83 raw), **57 OpenReview threads**, **368 reviewer comments**. 20 parallel discovery agents + 1 synthesis agent._

## Executive summary

CAFE already does the hard, distinctive work reviewers most respect (mechanical causality verifier, special-case unification, learned-not-tuned dials with per-dial ablation, MCAR/MAR/MNAR+block/subseq patterns, long-gap curves, CRPS/coverage with admitted over-conservatism, 12-seed significance, runtime/scaling, reported-vs-live downstream, registry caveat, HP disclosure). The surviving gaps cluster into five recurring reviewer themes that sank or bruised adjacent papers: (1) baseline breadth and currency—CAFE runs deep bidirectional models but does NOT run the closest CAUSAL/ONLINE statistical rivals it claims to subsume or supersede (SHASTA-PCA, MissNet, online robust tensor, NoTMF, FOCUS, online Gaussian-copula-EM/TGC, dynamic-factor-EM), which is the single most repeated and most acceptance-critical demand across the corpus; (2) the "no-hyperparameter / learned-not-tuned" claim is asserted but never stress-tested—internal constants (semantic harmonic set, g=N/(N+R), nu=4+6/kappa, inverse-variance fusion, window length) are exactly the kind of hidden dials reviewers repeatedly demanded sensitivity curves for, and several papers were dinged precisely for this; (3) scale and dataset diversity (all panels are similar mid-size sensor/macro tables, no high-dimensional or large-N stress, no clinical/healthcare benchmark like PhysioNet, no categorical/mixed-type data); (4) the conservative-interval admission is honest but a fix is now cheap and expected (conformal/quantile wrappers exist and were repeatedly cited as the rigorous answer); (5) theory altitude—CAFE proves point-in-time measurability but offers no consistency/recovery/convergence statement for the estimator itself, a recurring "no theory/identifiability" critique. The highest-leverage, most-tractable single action is to add a causal/online statistical-rival baseline panel; the highest-credibility theory action is a recovery/consistency sketch under the model's own generative assumptions.

## Ranked gaps to address (most important first)

Each item is a reviewer-style critique **not yet (or only partially) addressed** in CAFÉ, ranked by impact × recurrence × tractability. Status: `not-addressed` / `partial` / `admitted-future-work`.

### 1. Closest CAUSAL/ONLINE statistical rivals are claimed-subsumed but never run as baselines

- **Status in CAFÉ:** `partial` · **Impact:** high · **Effort:** high · **Theme:** weak/missing baselines
- **Critique (reviewer voice):** You run deep bidirectional models (SAITS/BRITS/Transformer/TimesNet/ImputeFormer) and a handful of cheap causal baselines, and you run BayOTIDE — but the methods that occupy your EXACT niche (online + low-rank + robust + factor-AR) are absent from the head-to-head: SHASTA-PCA (streaming heteroscedastic PPCA with missing data), MissNet (KDD'24 CPU SSM + dependency network on the same panels you target — and which you say you 'redesigned away from'), the 2025 online robust tensor recovery (online+robust+anomaly, three of your claims), NoTMF and FOCUS (low-rank dynamic factors + AR, your exact core), and online Gaussian-copula-EM / Temporal Gaussian Copula (TGC). Claiming to 'subsume the mechanics of this cluster' while only racing BayOTIDE invites the reviewer to suspect the omitted rivals would close the gap.
- **Why it matters:** Insufficient/dated baselines is the single most frequent major critique in the corpus and the proximate cause of multiple rejections; here it is sharpened because the missing methods are causal/online (your own ranking lane), so 'they're bidirectional, different game' does not excuse the omission.
- **Recommended action:** Add a 'causal/online statistical rivals' baseline block to the horse race: at minimum SHASTA-PCA, MissNet, the online robust tensor recovery, and NoTMF (online variant), run live on the same 8 panels and masks. Where a method is intrinsically batch (NoTMF, dynamic-factor-EM), run it under your right-edge-readout causal protocol and report the look-ahead Delta exactly as you do for deep models. State explicitly which CAFE by-products each rival lacks.
- **Recurs in:** Recurs in nearly every thread: TSI-Bench (mYVZ/uzuS 'missing recent methods', BRITS-beats-modern anomaly), GRIN (9yUs/JPtB/Sun: add low-rank MF, kriging, NAOMI, E2GAN, graph-MF), SSSD (5Tb4: apply strongest baselines everywhere), DiffImp (add GRIN/GATGPT), S4M (add GRU-ODE-Bayes/GraFITi/latent-ODE + linear-interp+PatchTST), MambaTS/SAMBA/S7 (must compare against the model you build on), HDDI (add PriSTI/GRIN), Conformal-PID (only ACI baseline). Adjacent papers SHASTA-PCA, MissNet, online-robust-tensor (2511.01267), NoTMF, FOCUS, TGC all exist and are CPU/online.

### 2. The 'no-hyperparameter / learned-not-tuned' claim has no sensitivity evidence for its internal constants

- **Status in CAFÉ:** `partial` · **Impact:** high · **Effort:** medium · **Theme:** hyperparameter sensitivity of 'no-HP' claim
- **Critique (reviewer voice):** You state 'no quantity in the objective is a constant fit to a benchmark,' yet the model carries several fixed internal choices: the semantic harmonic set {7,12,24,48,168,365}, the factor-vs-season gate g=N/(N+R), the kurtosis-to-nu map nu=4+6/kappa, the season-target winsorization (1.5+nu/4)*1.4826, the inverse-variance fusion weight, and the trailing-window length. These ARE hyperparameters by another name. A skeptical reviewer will ask: how sensitive are results to each, and what happens on data whose period is not in your fixed harmonic menu (you already concede aliasing when periods don't match)?
- **Why it matters:** 'Zero-config' is a headline selling point; an unsubstantiated no-HP claim is more dangerous than disclosed HPs because reviewers actively probe it. Demonstrating insensitivity converts a soft claim into a hard one; failing to address it lets a reviewer reframe your contribution as 'a model with hidden, untuned constants.'
- **Recommended action:** Add a one-page sensitivity appendix: tornado/line plots of mean causal MAE vs each internal constant (harmonic-set size, g, the nu map, window length, fusion weight) over the 8 panels, showing the result is flat in a wide band around the chosen value. Add a 'learned periods' robustness check where the harmonic menu is mismatched and quantify the aliasing penalty you already mention, so it is reported rather than discovered.
- **Recurs in:** TIDER (ICLR'23) 'biggest concern is many hyperparameters... unclear how chosen, how sensitive — wrong choices wipe out gains'; Conformal-PID 'many non-intuitive constants C_sat,K_I,eta; appendix shows choices matter enormously'; S4M/TimesNet/FSMamba all asked for systematic sensitivity (k, lambda, K1/K2/tau) across datasets, not one; recurring-demand-synthesis item (5) crisp-novelty/HP transparency.

### 3. Foundation-model imputers ('just use a TSFM') are not engaged

- **Status in CAFÉ:** `not-addressed` · **Impact:** high · **Effort:** medium · **Theme:** missing baselines / positioning vs current SOTA
- **Critique (reviewer voice):** The zero-config / no-per-dataset-tuning pitch now has a direct rival class: time-indexed foundation models that claim zero-shot imputation with no retraining (TabPFN-TS, MoTM, MOMENT, and the 2511.05980 33-dataset study). A reviewer in 2026 will ask why a practitioner would not just run a pretrained TSFM that also needs no tuning, and whether those models — run causally — beat CAFE.
- **Why it matters:** Leaving the most fashionable competing paradigm uncited reads as either unawareness or avoidance; it is the obvious 'have you compared to X' that an AC will forward. Pre-empting it cheaply protects the zero-config claim.
- **Recommended action:** Add a related-work paragraph distinguishing CAFE from imputation TSFMs (no pretraining corpus, no GPU, strictly causal, per-cell uncertainty + by-products) AND, if feasible, run MOMENT or a TabPFN-TS imputer under your causal right-edge protocol on 2-3 panels to show the look-ahead Delta and the compute gap. Even a cited zero-shot number on Beijing defuses the objection.
- **Recurs in:** Adjacent papers 2511.05980 (Are TSFMs the future of imputation?), MoTM (2507.13207), MOMENT (ICML'24), ImputeINR/MoTM continuous-modeling thread; corpus repeatedly demands newest baselines (SAMBA/MambaTS: LLM4TS, AutoTimes, GPT4TS; TSI-Bench: outdated models).

### 4. All datasets are similar mid-size panels; no high-dimensional / large-N or healthcare scale stress

- **Status in CAFÉ:** `partial` · **Impact:** high · **Effort:** high · **Theme:** more datasets / scale
- **Critique (reviewer voice):** The eight structured panels are all roughly comparable mid-size sensor/macro/energy tables (~10^2 columns, ~10^4 rows). There is no genuinely high-dimensional panel (1000s of channels), no large-N scale test, and no standard clinical benchmark (PhysioNet 2012) that this community treats as mandatory. - **Recommended action:** Add (a) one high-dimensional panel (>=1000 channels, e.g. an equity cross-section or M5) to exercise the N-scaling curve at a decade more width, and (b) PhysioNet-2012 (or a clinical EHR panel) under your causal protocol so the healthcare community can locate you. Report the N-scaling slope measured on the wide panel, not just the narrow ones.
- **Recurs in:** ProbTS (M4/M5/FRED/Monash demanded; 'do conclusions hold at larger scale?'); FSMamba (reviewers demanded high-channel data → authors added M5 3049ch, S&P500 1475ch); SPIN/GRIN ('beyond sensor networks'); DiffImp/HDDI/DiffPuter (Covertype 200k, larger datasets); MambaTS ('few datasets, hard to judge significance'). ### 5. Conservative intervals are admitted but a now-standard calibration fix (conformal/quantile) is neither applied nor benchmarked against

- **Status in CAFÉ:** `admitted-future-work` · **Impact:** high · **Effort:** medium · **Theme:** calibration sharpening
- **Critique (reviewer voice):** You honestly report over-coverage (+0.09 mean excess; Beijing 50%→94%) and defer sharpening to future work. But the field now has cheap, distribution-free wrappers that fix exactly this — split-conformal with missing values, conformalized matrix completion, mask-conditional/weighted conformal, and quantile sub-ensembles. A reviewer will ask why you did not simply wrap CAFE to get calibrated bands, and how your conservative PICP compares to a conformal baseline's guaranteed coverage.
- **Why it matters:** Calibration is repeatedly demanded as a first-class metric, and 'we admit it's wide' is weaker than 'we admit it and show a one-line recalibration recovers nominal coverage.' The fix is causally compatible (a trailing-window conformal layer is point-in-time) and would turn a conceded weakness into a strength.
- **Recommended action:** Add a causal split-conformal (trailing-window) recalibration layer over CAFE's per-cell sigma and report PICP/sharpness before vs after, plus a conformalized-matrix-completion baseline for distribution-free coverage. Show the recalibrated bands hit nominal while staying point-in-time. This is a sigma-only change, so imputation MAE is untouched.
- **Recurs in:** Conformal-with-missing-values (ICML'23), Conformalized Matrix Completion (NeurIPS'23), Weighted/mask-conditional conformal (2512.14221), Quantile Sub-Ensembles, CopulaCPTS/CoREL/Conformal-PID threads all center coverage+sharpness; CSDI reviewers demanded NLL; recurring 'superiority not established on coverage/width' (CoREL, CopulaCPTS).

### 6. No recovery/consistency/convergence theory for the estimator itself (only point-in-time measurability)

- **Status in CAFÉ:** `not-addressed` · **Impact:** high · **Effort:** high · **Theme:** theory / identifiability
- **Critique (reviewer voice):** Proposition 1 proves measurability/truncation-invariance — a causality property — but says nothing about whether the alternating-least-squares/EM fit converges, or whether CAFE recovers the true factors/loadings under its own generative model. The adjacent factor-model literature (Bai-Ng, TALL-PROJECT, dynamic-factor-EM, tensor-factor models) routinely supplies consistency and asymptotic normality. A statistics-leaning reviewer will note the gap between your statistical framing and the absence of any estimation guarantee.
- **Why it matters:** You market CAFE as a principled statistical model, not a heuristic; that framing raises the bar to at least a convergence statement or a recovery sketch. 'No theory' is a recurring major critique, and the comparison class here has theorems.
- **Recommended action:** Add a short theory paragraph: (a) state the monotone-descent / fixed-point convergence of the IRLS+ALS objective (Eq. obj is biconvex given nu,a), and (b) a recovery sketch — under the generative model (Eq. decomp/dyn) with bounded missingness, the online Kalman+ARD estimate of Wz_t is consistent as the trailing window grows, citing the Bai-Ng / dynamic-factor-EM (2502.04112) results CAFE's regime specializes. Even a clearly-scoped proposition + reference to the parent theorems closes the altitude gap.
- **Recurs in:** GRIN/MambaTS/FSMamba/SAMBA/S7 all hit with 'no theoretical justification / proof doesn't make sense / no convergence guarantee'; GINA/not-MIWAE/MissDiff/DiffPuter identifiability-and-convergence demands; adjacent JoE/Biometrika factor papers (2502.04112, 2403.13153, 1910.08273, 2103.03045, 2307.16370) all provide consistency/asymptotic-normality that CAFE lacks.

### 7. Baseline-tuning fairness for the deep models you run live is not documented

- **Status in CAFÉ:** `partial` · **Impact:** high · **Effort:** medium · **Theme:** baseline fairness / reproducibility
- **Critique (reviewer voice):** You run SAITS/BRITS/Transformer/TimesNet/ImputeFormer yourselves under a causal protocol and report that they 'collapse.' The obvious counter is that they collapse because they were not tuned/trained to convergence for YOUR data and protocol. Without a stated per-deep-model tuning/epoch/HP protocol, a reviewer will argue the right-edge-readout collapse is an artifact of under-training, not an intrinsic property.

- **Recommended action:** Add a baseline-protocol box: for each deep model state the source (PyPOTS official), epochs/early-stopping, the window length, and that bidirectional numbers reproduce published-range accuracy (proving they ARE adequately trained) so the causal collapse is demonstrably a protocol effect, not under-training. Show each deep model's bidirectional MAE matches its registry value within tolerance.
- **Recurs in:** TSI-Bench (uzuS 'BRITS beats modern — suboptimal tuning?'; per-method HPs absent); M3-Impute & MissDiff rejected over baselines worse than mean imputation / opaque tuning; MambaTS AC cited 'fidelity of baseline metrics when not trained to convergence'; SAMBA/MambaTS reviewers re-implemented baselines and got better numbers; DiffPuter/HDDI 'unclear if baselines tuned, official code or unified lib (PyPOTS)'.

### 9. Auto-router between joint and per-entity engines is admitted-missing, undercutting 'zero-config'

- **Status in CAFÉ:** `admitted-future-work` · **Impact:** medium · **Effort:** medium · **Theme:** failure-mode honesty / usability
- **Critique (reviewer voice):** You admit there is no automatic router between the joint and per-entity engines, and that the user must, in effect, know when each applies. That is in tension with the zero-config promise: the practitioner still has to make a structural choice, and the paper's 'which tool when' rule is human-applied, not automated.
- **Why it matters:** Reviewers probe the gap between a 'just-works' claim and residual manual choices; an honest, demonstrated auto-route (or a measured cost of getting it wrong) is far stronger than an admission. You already note the effective-rank readout signals when the factor assumption fails — that is the seed of an automatic switch.
- **Recommended action:** Either implement a one-line router keyed on the learned effective rank (rank→0 ⇒ switch to per-entity/random-walk prior) and show it recovers the right engine on FX vs structured panels automatically, or add a small table quantifying the MAE cost of choosing the wrong engine so the manual decision's stakes are explicit.
- **Recurs in:** TIDER 'how are choices made in practice'; ProbTS 'no quantifiable rule for which approach suits which dataset'; TimesNet/FSMamba scope-of-claim ('general' may be narrower); your own KNOWN-OPEN list names the missing router.

### 11. Cross-missingness / train-test mismatch generalization is not tested

- **Status in CAFÉ:** `partial` · **Impact:** medium · **Effort:** low · **Theme:** generalization across missingness
- **Critique (reviewer voice):** You sweep pattern x rate, but you do not show that dials learned under one missingness regime hold up when the deployment regime differs (e.g., learned on 10% point, evaluated on 50% block, or train/inference rate mismatch). For an 'adapts online, no per-dataset tuning' model this is the natural stress test.
- **Why it matters:** Cross-missingness robustness is explicitly demanded in the corpus and is a place where 'learned dials' could silently overfit the seen regime; demonstrating invariance strengthens the adaptivity claim.
- **Recommended action:** Add a small cross-regime matrix: estimate CAFE under regime A's missingness, evaluate under regime B (rate and pattern mismatched), and show MAE degrades gracefully because the dials are re-estimated online from the actual observed cells. This directly evidences the online-adaptation claim.
- **Recurs in:** SSSD (kCUh: 'train at 20% RM, test at 30%; train/inference condition mismatch'); DiffImp/HDDI inconsistent-grid critiques; M3-Impute 'robustness across missingness types and rates' and 'why better on some datasets'.

### 17. Runtime/scaling compared only to CPU baselines; the ~1000x compute-gap claim vs deep models is asserted, not measured side-by-side

- **Critique (reviewer voice):** You report CAFE's 1.16s and linear-T/sub-linear-N scaling against SoftImpute/TRMF, and assert a ~10^3x compute gap vs GPU deep imputers. ### 18. Fairness of the causal protocol: the right-edge-readout handicap on deep models may be argued unfair
